Deployment Strategy & Troubleshooting (12 Minutes)
You need to manage how a Deployment updates to avoid downtime.

Create a Deployment named app-v1 with 5 replicas of nginx:1.19.

Set the Strategy to RollingUpdate.

Configure maxUnavailable to 1 and maxSurge to 2.

Action: Update the image to nginx:1.21 and immediately pause the rollout.

Action: After 10 seconds, resume the rollout and check the status.

Verification: Use k rollout history deployment app-v1 to see the revision history.
